# Sharing and collaboration

A canvas can be shared two ways: a public read-only link, or an invite to another
Drawy user.

## Public view links

In **Share canvas**, turn on **Anyone with the link can view** and copy the link.
Anyone with it can open the canvas in a read-only view without an account. Turn
the toggle off to revoke it — the link stops working immediately.

## Inviting by username

Enter the person's Drawy username under **Share** and submit. They get edit
access straight away; there is nothing to accept, so the canvas appears in their
sidebar the next time it loads.

- The username must already exist.
- Only the owner can add or remove people.
- Adding the same person twice does nothing.

## Live collaboration

When several people have a canvas open, edits merge automatically and you see
each other's cursors, labelled with their username. Presence is live, so you can
tell who else is in the room.

## Version history

Drawy snapshots a canvas automatically, at most once every five minutes while it
is being edited, and keeps the newest 50 snapshots per canvas. Open **History**
in the canvas header to browse them.

Restoring a version replaces the canvas with that snapshot and records a new
snapshot first, so the state you restored from stays in the list.

Anyone who can view the canvas can open its history; only the owner and people
the canvas is shared with can restore a version.

## Permissions

| Role | View | Edit | Share | Delete |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Collaborator | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Public viewer | Yes | No | No | No |

## Revoking access

Open **Share canvas** and remove a collaborator, or switch the public link off.
Both take effect immediately.
